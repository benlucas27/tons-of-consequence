# Older innings: proposed verdicts (overnight run, 15 Oct 2026)

Branch `older-innings`, nothing pushed. For each of the highest-ranked hundreds with no match record (before 2006), the match facts from sources I opened and a proposed Consequence Test verdict. **You rule on each.** I could not open Cricinfo or ESPN (their terms forbid automated access), so most facts come from Wikipedia tour pages plus one more source where I found one.

**Done: 43 innings. Yes 25, no 13, cannot tell 5.** Not researched from the top-45 list: Patsy Hendren 205* (1930) and Jack Hobbs 123 (1921).

## What was hard
- Wikipedia's tour pages give totals, results and series order, but almost never the score when the batter came in, so many entries cannot say whether it was a rescue or a carry.
- "Series score going in" is usually worked out from the earlier results, not stated. Those cases are marked inferred.
- Where a hundred is in a drawn Test, the test only counts it if the team was in danger (behind or following on). Edrich, Gavaskar and McCabe pass only on the written rule, not on the feel of the match.
- Likely dead rubbers I found: Viv Richards 291 (1976), Bruce Mitchell 189* (1947), Mark Taylor 219 (1989), Joe Darling 160 (1898).

## Disagreements with our data (not changed)
- Herbert Sutcliffe 127 at Melbourne in 1925: the Wikipedia page gives 143 in the fourth Test and 127 in the second.
- Steve Waugh 200: Cricket Australia gives 3-73 when the Waughs came together; another account says 3-37.
- Brian Lara 153*: the target is given as 308, 310 and 311 by different sources.
- Pre-2006 rows where our dataset has no match result (most of these) get it from the page opened.
