## Rules that clear the flags before a person looks

1. A hundred that is marginal under the weight changes goes to review.
2. A tempo-only ton goes to review.
3. A win with the team already far ahead is kept if he made half the runs or scored at 90 or more per 100 balls (he put it out of reach); otherwise review.
4. A draw is kept if it stopped the other side taking the series lead; otherwise review.
5. A dead-rubber win by the series winners goes to review.
6. Partial evidence is kept only if it is a solid ton; otherwise review.

Result: of the 826 tons and marginal hundreds, **354** are cleared by rules and **472** go to manual review.
