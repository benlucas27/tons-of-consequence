Six batters review

Warner, Border, Hayden, Cook, Williamson and Stokes: every hundred checked, and the good tons most likely to swing to a ToC. Scores are the 13-law points (a hundred needs 45). My lean is a suggestion from the facts shown; the call is yours. Dates 29 Oct 2026.

What I checked across all six

  • Hundreds counted per batter: Warner 26, Border 27, Hayden 30, Cook 33, Williamson 33, Stokes 14. Every one has a verdict. The totals match the career figures I know; I did not re-read every scorecard.
  • Hayden's 380 v Zimbabwe (2003) is a good ton but his 101* v Zimbabwe the same year is a ToC. Law 13 takes points off hundreds against newer sides, so the 380 fits; the 101* is the one to look at.
  • Three were ToCs when you answered on 14 to 16 Oct, then the 13 laws turned them into good tons: Warner 102 Adelaide 2014, Warner 115 Centurion 2014 and Cook 235* Brisbane 2010. They are in the tables below.
  • Border's hundreds all rest on match reports (medium confidence). There is no ball-by-ball record before about 2006, so a drawn Test cannot be called a save without hand-checking reports.
