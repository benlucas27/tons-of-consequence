## 3. The 13 laws (signed off by me; the site's /tons-of-consequence page carries them)
1 (the only hard law) a ToC wins or saves a match: never in a loss; a dead rubber only if his side had won the series and the match; a draw only if his side was in danger of losing AND we have ball-by-ball (or high-confidence "several reports") proof the hundred saved it AND the opponent is tier 1 or 2 AND it was not weather or the clock. 2 the scoreboard when he came in (not for openers). 3 carrying the innings (share of runs, only hundred, clear of next batter; the rest of the team adding under 150 earns extra, 450+ costs a little). 4 part of a winning chase or close finish. 5 matters to the series. 6 pace. 7 the moment (tribute, farewell, comeback, emotional, Boxing Day/New Year). 8 no pressure (led by 200+) loses points. 9 opening platform. 10 late stand (7th wicket or later). 11 lower order (number 7 or lower). 12 an elite attack (career average under about 25). 13 who it was scored against (tier 4 minus 10, tier 3 minus 3; West Indies tier 2 to 2000, Sri Lanka tier 2 1996 to 2010). A hundred needs **45 points** (`THRESHOLD` in `src/lib/consequenceModel.js`). "Historic" (a first, a record) is a label only and must NOT change a verdict. The 600-run team total idea was tested and dropped.
