## Description (4000 max)
Not every Test hundred is created equal.

Tons of Consequence ranks every Test century ever scored, and tells you which ones were tons of consequence: the side needed the hundred, and it helped them win the match or avoid losing it. There are good tons, but not every ton is a ToC.

RANK THEM YOUR WAY
Every innings gets a Greatness score from six criteria: Match Impact, Clutchness, Narrative, Conditions & Opposition, Historical Weight and Perceived Greatness. Put them in the order you care about, or start from a ready-made point of view: Purist, Analyst, Patriot or Romantic. Watch the rankings reshuffle.

THE ToC LEADERBOARD
Search any batter with a Test hundred and see how many of their hundreds were ToCs, with the reason for every one and a plain note when one can't be measured yet. Sort by most ToCs, highest share, best single innings or team win rate.

COMPARE
Put any two innings head to head, criterion by criterion, and see whether the verdict holds under every point of view. Or compare whole careers: The Big Four, The Openers, Ashes Heroes and more.

ASK THE RANKINGS
Type a question: "Best centuries by India since 2010", "Lara 153 vs Stokes 135", "Matt Renshaw's 190".

MORE
The Pub Test works out how you rank a hundred in eight questions. Women's Tests have their own ToCs. Hello Cricket collects the moments the Hello Sport hosts talk tons, with timestamps. Every verdict shows its sources and how firm the evidence is.

SHARE AND GET ALERTS
Share any innings or leaderboard as an image. Switch on alerts and get a notification when a new ToC is added.

WORKS OFFLINE
The whole database is on your phone, and updates itself when you're online.

An independent project, not affiliated with any cricket board. The scores and verdicts are opinion, with the facts and sources shown.
