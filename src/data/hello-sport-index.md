# Hello Sport -- Test innings commentary index

Timestamped links to every segment where Tom and Eddy (Hello Sport podcast) talk about a specific Test century, plus the origin of "tons of consequence".

**Status:** v2. Cricket-season episodes scanned: 82 of 89 (Oct 2024 - Oct 2026). The other 7 have no YouTube transcript (see the end of this file). Every century row has been checked against the Tons of Consequence database and carries its `toc_id`.

## How to use this file

- Each row in **Entries** is one segment. Link it to the innings page using `toc_id` (the database innings id).
- `start` is where the chat about that innings begins, so use it as the preview start point.
- `link` is the YouTube URL with `&t=<seconds>s` already set.
- `type` is one of:
  - `innings`: a specific knock. Tag it to that innings.
  - `concept`: tons of consequence framework chat. Use it on the about/methodology page.
  - `career`: career tallies, not one innings. Tag it to the player page.
  - `historic`: an older innings referenced in passing.
- Timestamps are from the YouTube versions. The audio podcast versions can be offset slightly by ad reads.
- **Change since v1:** a `toc_id` column has been added at the end of the table. The v1 rows are unchanged apart from confirmed scores. The new rows are marked **NEW** in the note.

## Origin: tons of consequence

Episode #828, published 12 Jan 2026 straight after the Sydney Test. The YouTube title is "#828 - Recapping Our Sydney Ashes Victory...". The podcast title is "2026: Year Of The Punter & Dribbler".

- **33:06** Joe Root chat begins. He has two tons this series and is level with Ponting on 41 Test tons.
- **34:28** "Tons of consequence" is named: the idea of separating tons from tons *of consequence*.
- **35:13 - 35:51** Worked examples. A hundred made under zero pressure is "a good ton", but not a ton of consequence.
- **36:51** Listener call-out: if you've got a formula or a way of deciphering it, "might be something in it for you". It must be "third party tested".
- **37:11** Head's and Smith's dead-rubber Sydney tons are ruled tons of consequence.

Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KAZuw748xiw&t=2068s
