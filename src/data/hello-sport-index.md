# Hello Sport -- Test innings commentary index

Timestamped links to every segment where Tom and Eddy (Hello Sport podcast) talk about a specific Test innings, plus the origin of "tons of consequence".

**Status:** partial. 8 episodes reviewed. The remaining ~80 cricket-season episodes (Oct 2024 - Oct 2026) are still to be scanned, and results will be appended to the Entries table in the same format.

## How to use this file

- Each row in **Entries** is one segment. Tag it to the innings page using `player` + `match`.
- `start` is where the chat about that innings begins. Use it as the preview start point.
- `link` is the YouTube URL with `&t=<seconds>s` already set.
- `type` is one of:
  - `innings` -- discussion of a specific knock. Tag it to that innings.
  - `concept` -- tons of consequence framework chat. Use it for the about/methodology page.
  - `career` -- career tallies or records, not one innings. Tag it to the player page.
  - `historic` -- older innings referenced in passing.
- Scores and match details marked `(verify)` come from the hosts' own words in auto-captions. Check them against the innings database before display.
- Timestamps are from the YouTube versions. The audio podcast versions can be offset slightly by ad reads.

## Origin: tons of consequence

Episode #828, published 12 Jan 2026 straight after the Sydney Test. YouTube title: "#828 - Recapping Our Sydney Ashes Victory...". Podcast title: "2026: Year Of The Punter & Dribbler".

- **33:06** -- The Joe Root chat starts. He has two tons this series and is level with Ponting on 41 Test tons.
- **34:28** -- "Tons of consequence" is named as the idea of separating tons from tons *of consequence*.
- **35:13 - 35:51** -- Worked examples. A hundred made under zero pressure is "a good ton", but not a ton of consequence.
- **36:51** -- The listener call-out. If you've got a formula or a way of deciphering it, "might be something in it for you". It needs to be third party tested.
- **37:11** -- Head's and Smith's dead-rubber Sydney tons are ruled tons of consequence.

Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KAZuw748xiw&t=2068s
